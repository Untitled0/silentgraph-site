About

SILENTGRAPH is an independent design studio.

It is the parent studio behind fonica, a silver jewelry brand, and Lapis Razli, a music project. We design fonica's pieces, brand site, and artwork — and Lapis Razli's artwork and videos.

We also build our own digital products: tools for makers. Objects (fonica), sound (Lapis Razli), and tools (Digital Products) — three outputs, one design philosophy.

For contact, see the Support page.
