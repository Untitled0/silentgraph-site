Privacy Policy

This policy applies to the apps and websites of SILENTGRAPH ("the Studio"). If a product handles data differently, that is stated in the product or on its page.

1. Information we collect

Our apps do not collect personal information. Data an app handles — settings, and the data you create — is stored only on your device. It is never sent to our servers.

2. Sharing with third parties

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties.

3. Advertising and tracking

Our apps contain no third-party advertising SDKs and do no ad tracking. This website does not use analytics tracking.

4. External services

Apps are distributed through platforms such as the App Store and Google Play. See each platform's privacy policy for how information is handled there.

5. Contact

Questions about this policy: see the Support page (contact channel in preparation).

6. Changes

This policy may change without notice. Changes take effect when posted on this page.
